Integrations

Your inventory, wherever it lives.

Dealers ask us one question first: “Does DealerEdge work with my DMS?” The honest answer is yes — because we built four ways in, and one of them works for every dealership we've met.

Four Ways In

Four ways to connect
your DMS.

01

Native DMS feeds

DealerEdge ingests dealer management system inventory feeds directly — WinBoats and Lightspeed feeds sync automatically today. A new unit entered in your DMS shows up as a complete, sales-ready listing the same day, and only changes are processed on each sync, so nothing goes stale.

02

Any DMS with a feed export

Run Dockmaster, DX1, Blackpurl, IDS Astra, or another system? If your DMS can export inventory — XML or CSV, scheduled or on demand — DealerEdge normalizes it into the same 65+ field unit record we build from native feeds: year, make, model, price, engine, hours, options, colors, HIN, location, and everything else your DMS knows about the boat. Setup is part of onboarding; you don't build anything.

03

Secure file-drop

No API, no scheduled export? Your team (or your DMS vendor) drops the inventory file into a private, secure folder and DealerEdge picks it up and syncs automatically. This is live in production today — it's how some of our feeds run.

04

No export at all? We onboard from your website.

This is the one nobody else offers: if your inventory already lives on your current website, DealerEdge can read it straight from your site — every unit, specs, and photos — and build your new inventory from there. No DMS project, no IT ticket, no data entry. It's how we can baseline a dealership before a contract is even signed.

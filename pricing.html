Contract

Contract terms,
plainly.

How long is the contract?

Month-to-month by default. Choose an annual agreement and we waive the one-time onboarding fee.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime — there is no long-term lock-in and no cancellation fee.

What happens when I cancel?

You keep your website and your data. Your site is exported and deployed to your own hosting account and keeps working, and your leads, customers, inventory, and conversation history come with you as a full machine-readable export.

What's always pass-through?

Third-party ad spend, premium link-building budgets, and AI voice minutes beyond your plan's included 1,000 are billed at cost — never marked up into the subscription. We say this plainly because fee-stacking is the thing dealers hate most about legacy vendors.

What support is included?

Every tier includes 24/7 AI-assisted support and human support on business hours. Response commitments are published on our support page.

What does it replace?

For less than the cost of one junior employee, a DealerEdge subscription replaces your web vendor, your SEO agency, your ad agency, and your after-hours answering service — one platform, one monthly price.

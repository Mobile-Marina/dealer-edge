The Headline

16 → 636 monthly form submissions

Before DealerEdge, Premier's site produced 16 form submissions in a month. After launch, the same period produced 636 — a 3,875% increase. Those weren't empty clicks: 184 boat inquiry forms, plus schedule-a-visit, text-us, and finance requests, feeding 101 CRM leads captured since launch (31 phone clicks, 15 texts, 10 chats, 10 scheduled visits).

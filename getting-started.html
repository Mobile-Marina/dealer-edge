Getting Started · Onboarding & Setup

“How long does setup take?”

Short answer: your new site is typically live within the first two weeks, and the full platform — AI follow-up, marketing, reporting — is fully running within 30 days. Your team's time investment is a kickoff call, a review pass, and training. Here's the whole implementation, step by step.

The implementation timeline

Day 1 to Day 30,
step by step.

Day 1

Kickoff & connect

One call. We connect your inventory source — a native DMS feed, a feed export, secure file-drop, or straight from your current website if your DMS has no export — and collect brand assets (logo, locations, team). No IT project on your side; setup of the data connection is our job, not yours.

Week 1

We build, you review

The AI builds your site: every unit pulled in, 65+ fields normalized per boat, listing copy written, photos enhanced, category and location pages generated, schema and SEO foundation in place. You get a full preview and a review pass — changes go live in under 48 hours.

Weeks 1–2 · Go live

Website live

Your new site launches, typically within the first two weeks of kickoff. From this point, new units entered in your DMS appear as complete listings the same day, and sold boats come down everywhere automatically.

Once you're live

AI goes to work Growth and Dominance

AI Customer Connect switches on: 24/7 website chat and SMS/email auto-response with first touch in under 90 seconds. Nurture streams, unified inbox, and — on Growth+ — AI-managed Google and Meta advertising begin. For the first two weeks after launch, our team runs daily white-glove monitoring on every new dealership: a human reviews your leads, your AI conversations, and your site performance every day.

Days 15–30

Training, tuning, first report

Your team gets trained on the dashboard — leads, inventory management, content, reporting. We tune lead routing and follow-up to how your store actually sells, and you receive your first monthly performance report with your baseline established, so every month after is measured against something real.
